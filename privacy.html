Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Information We Collect

こなごな (Konagona) does not collect any personal information. Your settings, such as the button text you choose, and the number of times you have played are stored only on your device. No account registration is required, and no data is transmitted to our servers or any external server operated by us.

Network Communication

The app works entirely on your device and does not communicate with any server, except for in-app purchases processed by Apple.

Advertising and Analytics

The app does not display advertisements and does not use any analytics or tracking tools.

Third-Party Services

こなごな uses Apple's StoreKit for in-app purchases. Apple's privacy policy applies to any purchase transactions.
